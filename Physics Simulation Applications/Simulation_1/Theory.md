# Physics Theory Test

## Transmon Hamiltonian

$$
H = 4E_C(\hat n-n_g)^2 - E_J\cos(\hat\phi)
$$

## Energy Eigenstates

$$
H|j\rangle = E_j|j\rangle
$$

## Transition Frequency

$$
f_{j,j+1} = \frac{E_{j+1}-E_j}{h}
$$

## Anharmonicity

$$
\alpha = f_{12}-f_{01}
$$

## Population

$$
P_j(t)=\langle j|\rho(t)|j\rangle
$$

## Lindblad Equation

$$
\frac{d\rho}{dt}
=
-\frac{i}{\hbar}[H(t),\rho]
+
\sum_k
\left(
L_k\rho L_k^\dagger
-
\frac{1}{2}
\{L_k^\dagger L_k,\rho\}
\right)
$$

## Neural Operator Mapping

$$
\mathcal{G}:u(t)\longrightarrow\mathbf{P}(t)
$$
